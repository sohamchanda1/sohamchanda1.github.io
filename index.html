Research

Research interests

My research lies in symplectic and contact topology. I use tools inspired by symplectic field theory to study how changing fillings affects Floer-theoretic invariants. This approach lets me use contact-geometric data of Legendrians to obtain results about Lagrangian manifolds, and conversely.

  • Symplectic topology
  • Contact topology
  • Floer theory
  • Legendrians
  • Lagrangian submanifolds
